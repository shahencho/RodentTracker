Validation against a published reference

Data: 14 open-field videos from Sturman et al. 2020 (Zenodo 3608658, CC BY 4.0) that our detector never saw in training. Reference: the DLCAnalyzer analysis from the same paper (DeepLabCut body-centre track, 42 × 42 cm arena, the first 100 and last 250 frames removed, centre = middle 50% of the side). Our Python port of DLCAnalyzer reproduces the paper's published example (OFT_3) exactly.
